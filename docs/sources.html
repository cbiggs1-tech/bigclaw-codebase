Style Intelligence & AI Reasoning

Style Gate Engine
Deterministic pre-buy filters per portfolio defined by the April 2026 four-LLM thesis debate. Authoritative — a ticker either passes or fails. No AI override layer. Failures are rejected; the 20-dim scoring engine handles dynamic re-evaluation when metrics improve later.
style_gates.py Zero Cost
Candidate Discovery Screener
Weekly Finviz screening with portfolio-specific filters, then deterministic IPS gate filtering. Top survivors enter the candidate universe (max 10 per portfolio, matching the holding cap) and compete against current holdings via best-in-class score-based rotation.
candidate_screener.py Saturday 9 AM CT
LLM Token Surveillance
Daily aggregator over logs/llm_calls.jsonl. Posts per-script token+cost breakdown to Slack at 6 AM CT. Alerts if any single script exceeds 1M tokens or $5 in one day — catches a new heavy consumer before it bills hundreds undetected.
llm_token_report.py Daily 6 AM CT
