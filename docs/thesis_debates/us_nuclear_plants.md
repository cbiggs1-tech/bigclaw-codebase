### Entergy (Private — sold nuclear fleet; see below)

> Entergy divested its merchant nuclear fleet to Holtec and others. As of early 2026, Entergy retains **no operating commercial nuclear plants**. Its regulated utility nuclear plants (Grand Gulf, River Bend, Waterford) were transferred to **Entergy's regulated subsidiaries** which remain part of Entergy Corporation.
